# Ethics & responsible use

LAoN (Lost Ancient Arts of Nepal) is an independent open-source project. It helps people **discover possible visual matches** between documented Nepali cultural objects and public museum records. It does **not** determine legal ownership and does **not** file restitution claims.

## Principles

1. **Possible match ≠ legal claim**  
   Similarity scores and ranked results are research aids only. Only authorized bodies (e.g. the Government of Nepal / Department of Archaeology and their counsel) can pursue formal claims.

2. **Do not harass institutions or people**  
   Do not use LAoN outputs to publicly accuse museums, dealers, or collectors without human expert review and proper channels. False positives are expected.

3. **Soft language by default**  
   UI and exports use phrasing such as *“possible visual match”* and *“not a determination of legal ownership.”* Avoid absolute labels like “stolen from X” unless the catalog record is explicitly sourced that way and reviewed.

4. **Respect copyright and licenses**  
   - Prefer CC0 / public-domain museum images (e.g. Met Open Access, Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access).  
   - Do not bulk-publish copyrighted book plates (e.g. Bangdel, Schick) in the public repo.  
   - Private research corpora stay on the operator’s machine (`PRIVATE_CORPUS_PATH`); they are never committed to git.

5. **Credit sources**  
   Cite scholarly works, museum accession pages, news of repatriations, and community contributors. LAoN is not affiliated with NHRC, Lost Arts of Nepal, HAR, Global Nepali Museum, or any museum.

6. **Human review for high-stakes matches**  
   Before amplifying a match that could affect a living community or a formal case, seek expert review (iconography, measurements, damage marks, provenance).

7. **Community photos with consent**  
   Contributions intended for the public catalog require an explicit open license (recommended: CC BY 4.0) and consent to publish.

## Graded actions (not “file a claim”)

| Action | Intent |
|---|---|
| Save match | Private notes for the user |
| Share match | Public link with score + disclaimer |
| Report package | User-exported evidence JSON/summary they can send to whoever they choose |
| Expert review (later) | Community/experts refine confidence |

## Scope

Nepal-first. Global “country packs” may come later under the same ethics rules. Expanding geography does not expand legal authority.
